Playing needs a browser wallet on Robinhood mainnet holding a hardwired Rare Friends Generations NFT (generation ≥ 1). The FriendSDK checks ownership before play. This page is only a showcase: it never connects a wallet.

Every family plays differently

Your Friend's Generations family sets its signature ability (R), a passive trait and its voice. The clips show each signature cast on the sample Friend. In the game, your own Friend casts it with its own on-chain artwork.

Your Friend's seed shapes the rest

The Friend's on-chain art seed picks its attack, bolt, dodge and action-bar look, so the same Friend always gets the same kit. Across the nine families that makes 5,184 combinations.

        Thirty floors, ten acts

        Every act is its own place, with its own palette, props, creatures and composed creepy tune. A titled guardian blocks the stairs on floors without a boss.

        Ten bosses

        One at the bottom of every act, each with its own pixel art, attacks and phases. The first time each one falls, it leaves you a pocket-sized pet of itself.

        Dress your Friend, restore the camp

        RF you earn in the dungeon has somewhere to go: 70+ cosmetics in six categories, a Blessing Shrine for your next descent, and a camp to rebuild. Everything here is simulated RF.
